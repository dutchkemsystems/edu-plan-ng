# Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council (NERDC) Curriculum Sources

---

## 1. Statutory Background

The Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council (NERDC) is the apex government agency established under Nigerian federal law to design, update, and regulate national curricula for basic and secondary education.

---

## 2. Curriculum Coverage in EduPlan NG

EduPlan NG incorporates the approved national syllabus structure across:
- **Junior Secondary School (JSS 1–3 / Basic 7–9)**:
  - Foundational preparation for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE / Junior WAEC).
  - Core subjects: Mathematics, English Studies, Basic Science & Technology, Civic Education.
- **Senior Secondary School (SSS 1–3)**:
  - Terminal secondary preparation for the West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE) administered by WAEC, and the Senior School Certificate Examination (SSCE) administered by NECO.
  - Core subjects: General Mathematics, English Language, Biology, Economics.

---

## 3. Weekly Structure & Assessment Guidelines

- 3 Terms per Academic Session (1st Term: Sept–Dec, 2nd Term: Jan–April, 3rd Term: May–July).
- 13 Instructional Weeks per Term:
  - Weeks 1–6: Modular Concept Explication & Laboratory Experiments
  - Week 7: Mid-Term Continuous Assessment (CA 1) & Mid-Term Break
  - Weeks 8–11: Advanced Theoretical & Practical Synthesis
  - Week 12: Comprehensive Termly Revision
  - Week 13: Terminal Promotional Examination & Broadsheet Compilation
