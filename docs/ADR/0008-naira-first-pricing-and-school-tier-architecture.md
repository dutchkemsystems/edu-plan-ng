# ADR 0008: Naira-First Pricing Model & School Tier Architecture

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-09-18
- **Deciders**: Product, Strategy & Commercial Team

## Context
Pricing software in USD creates severe conversion friction for Nigerian teachers due to exchange rate volatility and CBN foreign currency card limits. Nigerian teachers expect clear Naira amounts (NGN), annual school discounts, and group licensing for school administrations.

## Decision
We established a Naira-first pricing model:
- **Free**: 5 lesson plans/month (adoption hook).
- **Pro Monthly**: ₦2,500/month.
- **Pro Annual**: ₦22,000/year (Save 27%, effective ₦1,833/month).
- **School License**: ₦45,000/year for 10 teachers with HOD audit dashboard.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Eliminates FX friction, enables school-wide institutional purchasing, aligns with Nigerian academic school term cycles, and generates predictable recurring revenue.
- **Negative**: Pricing must be reviewed periodically against domestic inflation trends.
