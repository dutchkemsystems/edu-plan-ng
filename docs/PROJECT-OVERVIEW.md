# Project Overview — EduPlan NG

## Executive Summary
EduPlan NG is an acquisition-grade lesson planning, curriculum browsing, and academic operations platform built specifically for Nigeria's 1.5M+ secondary school teachers across Junior Secondary (JSS 1–3) and Senior Secondary (SSS 1–3).

## Problem Solved
1. **Manual Scheme of Work Preparation**: Nigerian teachers traditionally spend 15–20 hours at the start of every 13-week term manually copying and transcribing NERDC syllabus breakdowns into paper diaries.
2. **Pedagogical Alignment Gaps**: National inspections frequently flag lesson plans lacking measurable behavioral objectives or failing Bloom's cognitive taxonomy benchmarks.
3. **Connectivity Fragility**: Intermittent broadband in suburban and rural schools prevents reliable cloud-only tool usage.
4. **FX Payment Blockers**: Dollar-denominated software fails due to domestic debit card limits and volatile exchange rates.

## The Solution
EduPlan NG eliminates these barriers with:
- Instant 1-click generation of NERDC-compliant 13-week Schemes of Work.
- An intelligent lesson plan composer with 20s autosave and Bloom's cognitive taxonomy validation.
- Native WhatsApp and SMS integration for direct parent communications in English, Yorùbá, Igbo, and Hausa.
- Seamless domestic Naira subscriptions via Kora Pay (Card, Transfer, USSD, OPay).
