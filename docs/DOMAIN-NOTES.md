# Nigerian Education Domain Knowledge & Pedagogical Specifications

## The Secondary Education Structure in Nigeria

1. **Junior Secondary School (JSS 1–3 / Basic 7–9)**:
   - Caters to students aged approximately 11 to 14.
   - Concludes with the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE), also colloquially known as "Junior WAEC".
   - Curriculum governed strictly by the NERDC Basic Education Curriculum guidelines.

2. **Senior Secondary School (SSS 1–3)**:
   - Caters to students aged approximately 14 to 17 across Science, Humanities/Arts, Commercial, and Technical streams.
   - Concludes with two major regional examinations:
     - **WASSCE** (West African Senior School Certificate Examination) administered by the West African Examinations Council (WAEC).
     - **SSCE** (Senior School Certificate Examination) administered by the National Examinations Council (NECO).

## The 13-Week Term Progression

Nigerian academic terms run across 13 weeks:
- **Week 1**: Resumption test, holiday assignment review, and introductory concept explication.
- **Weeks 2–6**: Core syllabus topics, classwork drills, and laboratory experiments.
- **Week 7**: Mid-Term Continuous Assessment (CA 1) test and Mid-Term Break (including Open Day).
- **Weeks 8–11**: Advanced concept integration and practical applications.
- **Week 12**: Comprehensive termly revision and mock practice.
- **Week 13**: Terminal Promotional Examination, script grading, and Broadsheet compilation.

## Nigerian Grading Systems

### WAEC Standard (SSS 1–3)
- **A1** (75–100%): Distinction
- **B2** (70–74%): Very Good
- **B3** (65–69%): Good
- **C4** (60–64%): Credit
- **C5** (55–59%): Credit
- **C6** (50–54%): Credit
- **D7** (45–49%): Pass
- **E8** (40–44%): Pass
- **F9** (0–39%): Fail
